Bridging research and real-world systems

Georbit is an autonomous, independent nonprofit association deeply engaged in research. Through faculty-led laboratories, open-source work, and peer-reviewed publications, we pursue rigorous, verifiable research across intelligent technologies and systems.

Advancing open research and talent

Our community of faculty researchers and fellows advances cutting-edge research, from fundamental machine learning and safety intelligence to applied distributed systems. Through labs, fellowships, and internships, we enable researchers driving innovation worldwide.
