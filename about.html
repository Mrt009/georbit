About Georbit

Georbit is an autonomous, independent nonprofit association deeply engaged in research. We turn open scientific inquiry into verifiable implementations through faculty-led laboratories, open-source work, and peer-reviewed publications.

Where research breakthroughs turn into real-world impact

We drive open science and verifiable research across natural language processing, cryptography, distributed systems, and applied AI. Our faculty-led laboratories produce peer-reviewed publications, open-source frameworks, and practical engineering tools that deliver measurable impact.

What we do

Research & Open Science

Georbit advances the frontiers of computing through faculty-led laboratories spanning NLP, cryptography, networks, and applied AI. Our researchers tackle real-world challenges in industrial safety intelligence, disaster risk prediction, and secure communications, while publishing every line of code under open-source licenses. We ensure the research ecosystem has both breakthrough discoveries and verifiable implementations.

Talent & Fellowships

We nurture the next generation of researchers through two fellowship tracks — Research With Us for faculty and postdocs, and Research Under Us for students and early-career researchers. Our fellows receive GPU compute allocations, publication support, open-source mentorship, and direct access to faculty laboratory leads. From undergraduate researchers to postdoctoral scholars, we create pathways for talent at every stage.

Georbit's impact

As an autonomous, independent nonprofit association, Georbit is committed to open, reproducible research. Based in New Delhi, with faculty partnerships at Jamia Millia Islamia, Georbit's four operational laboratories produce peer-reviewed publications, open-source systems, and patent filings. Our community connects researchers, fellows, and collaborators across institutions worldwide.
