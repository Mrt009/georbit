Two fellowship tracks for researchers at every stage — from undergraduate students to postdoctoral scholars.

Georbit Research Institute

FellowshipTracks

Two complementary research pathways at Georbit — for independent collaboration and guided research development.

Track A

Research With Us

For faculty, postdoctoral scholars, and senior researchers who want to collaborate directly with Georbit's laboratories.

  • Co-author preprints and papers
  • Access to GPU compute allocations
  • Direct mentorship from laboratory leads
  • Joint IP and open-source contributions
  • 6–12 month engagement, renewable
Explore Track A →
Track B

Research Under Us

For students, early-career researchers, and associates who want structured mentorship and guided research experience.

  • Structured research internship
  • Weekly mentorship sessions
  • Contribute to active research projects
  • Letter of recommendation on completion
  • 3–6 month engagement with extension option
Explore Track B →
