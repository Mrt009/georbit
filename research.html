Core Research Areas

Georbit's research spans four strategic areas driving advances in applied computing. Our faculty-led laboratories combine theoretical depth with open-source, production-ready implementations that deliver measurable impact across industry and academia.

OUR RESEARCH LABS

Focused Research for Real-World Impact

LAB-TA

NLP & Text Mining for Industrial Intelligence

We build domain-specific language models and text mining frameworks for industrial safety, academic analysis, and information retrieval, producing reproducible, production-grade NLP pipelines.

Led by Prof. Tanvir Ahmad at Jamia Millia Islamia

LAB-MA

Chaos-Based Cryptographic Systems

We investigate chaotic S-Box construction, multimedia encryption, and cryptographic primitives for secure communication, privacy, and cloud security.

Led by Prof. Musheer Ahmad (LAB-MA)

LAB-AM

Wireless Networks & Distributed Systems

We work on mobile ad-hoc networks (MANETs), IoT security, and wireless communication to improve reliability, routing efficiency, and decentralized systems.

Led by Prof. Mohammad Amjad (LAB-AM)

LAB-SG

Applied AI & Deep-Tech Engineering

We develop production-grade AI systems, from computer vision to real-time inference and cloud-native ML deployment, bridging research prototypes and scalable systems.

Led by Sachin Gaur (LAB-SG), ex-CERN
